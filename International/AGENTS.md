Before releasing an installer, use REGRESSION_CHECKLIST.md and save a separate
report under validation/ with version/build, OS/architecture, source commit,
final executable SHA-256 and evidence for each applicable result. FAIL and
NOT TESTED do not count as PASS. Unpassed required live checks block a stable
release. CI candidates and draft changes must be identified as such. Never
publish or restore the cancelled Golos 2.0 archive. Preserve previous releases.
